## Tour Guide Sarajevo (TGS)

### Site brief

Tour Guide Sarajevo is the blog of a family-run local guiding company in Sarajevo, Bosnia and Herzegovina. It writes for visitors planning a trip to Sarajevo and central Bosnia, and for travelers already in the city looking for something to do, eat or understand.

Topics: Sarajevo's old town and sights (Baščaršija, Sebilj, Gazi Husrev-beg Mosque, the Old Orthodox Church, the Catholic Cathedral and the Synagogue, Vijećnica, the Latin Bridge, the Yellow and White Fortresses); the city's history, from the Ottoman and Austro-Hungarian eras to the 1914 assassination of Archduke Franz Ferdinand, the 1984 Winter Olympics and the 1992–1996 siege (the Tunnel of Hope, Sniper Alley, Gallery 11/07/95, the War Childhood Museum); Sarajevo as a meeting place of faiths and cultures; Mount Trebević, the cable car and the Olympic bobsled track; Vrelo Bosne and Ilidža; the mountains for skiing and hiking (Jahorina, Bjelašnica, Igman) and the highland village of Lukomir; Bosnian food and coffee culture (ćevapi, burek and pita, Bosnian coffee, sweets, the old craft streets); festivals and holidays (Sarajevo Film Festival in August, Ramadan and Bajram, Christmas and Easter of both churches, Baščaršija Nights, Sarajevo Winter); practical tips (getting there by air, train and bus, the Sarajevo–Mostar train, money and cards, when to go, respectful visits to mosques and churches, winter driving); and day trips from Sarajevo (Mostar, Konjic and Jablanica, Travnik and Jajce, Visoko, the Srebrenica Memorial Center).

Voice: a friendly local guide born in Sarajevo. Warm, practical and specific, with real places, names, seasons and travel times. Explain history, including the war and the siege, clearly and respectfully, centred on the people of the city, without taking political sides. Write about the Srebrenica genocide and other wartime events factually and with care, as the courts and memorials record them.

Where it fits naturally, point readers to the company's guided tours and its personalized tour planning service, naming only tours that are listed on the site. Do not invent prices, schedules or opening hours. Avoid generic travel clichés, current politics beyond historical context, and topics with no link to Sarajevo and Bosnia and Herzegovina.

### Trend countries

BA, HR, TR, DE, AT, GB, US, SA, AE, KW. Turkey and the Gulf states send many of Sarajevo's visitors.

## Tour Guide Medjugorje (TGMe)

### Site brief

Tour Guide Medjugorje is the blog of a family-run local guiding company in Medjugorje, Bosnia and Herzegovina. It writes for pilgrims and visitors planning a trip to Medjugorje and Herzegovina, and for those already in the village looking for guidance, a quiet place to pray or a day out.

Topics: the pilgrimage itself (St. James Church and the evening program, Mass in many languages, adoration and confession, Apparition Hill (Podbrdo) and its rosary path, Cross Mountain (Križevac) and the Stations of the Cross, the Blue Cross, the Risen Christ statue, the Mother's Village and the community's charitable work); the history of Medjugorje since 1981, the visionaries and the place's growth into a world pilgrimage destination; the Church's position, told accurately (pilgrimages authorized by Pope Francis in 2019, and the Holy See's 2024 note granting a "nihil obstat" to the spiritual experience while making no ruling on the apparitions); the main dates (the anniversary on 24–25 June, the Youth Festival Mladifest in early August, Christmas, Easter and the Feast of the Assumption); practical tips for pilgrims (getting there from the Split, Dubrovnik, Mostar and Sarajevo airports, where to stay, the climbs in summer heat, shoes and early starts, dress and quiet at holy places, visiting with older people or children, money and cards); local life and food in Herzegovina (Brotnjo and Čitluk wines such as Žilavka and Blatina, home cooking, family-run guesthouses); and day trips (Mostar and the Old Bridge, Kravice waterfalls, Počitelj, Blagaj Tekke, Široki Brijeg Franciscan monastery, Hutovo Blato, Dubrovnik).

Voice: a friendly local guide who grew up in Medjugorje. Warm, calm, practical and specific, with real places, names, seasons and walking times. Write respectfully for believers and with care for every visitor: describe the faith and the experiences of pilgrims honestly, without presenting the apparitions as proven fact and without dismissing them. Explain the region's history, including the 1990s war, clearly and without taking political sides.

Where it fits naturally, point readers to the company's guided tours, pilgrimage support and personalized tour planning service, naming only tours that are listed on the site. Do not invent prices, schedules, Mass times or opening hours. Avoid sensational or commercial treatment of faith, claims of miracles or healings presented as fact, Church controversies beyond the official position, current politics, and topics with no link to Medjugorje and Herzegovina.

### Seed terms

Medjugorje, Medjugorje pilgrimage, Apparition Hill, Cross Mountain Medjugorje, Krizevac, Podbrdo, St James Church Medjugorje, Blue Cross Medjugorje, Risen Christ statue, Medjugorje Youth Festival, Mladifest, Medjugorje anniversary, Our Lady of Medjugorje, Queen of Peace, Medjugorje message, Mother's Village, Citluk, Zilavka, Blatina, Mostar, Kravice, Pocitelj, Blagaj, Siroki Brijeg, Hutovo Blato, Herzegovina, Medjugorje day trips, Bosnia and Herzegovina

### Trend countries

BA, HR, IT, IE, PL, US, GB, DE, AT, FR. Pilgrims come mostly from Italy, Ireland, Poland, the US and the German-speaking countries.
